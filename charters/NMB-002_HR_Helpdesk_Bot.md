# Project Charter: HR Helpdesk Bot (NMB-002)

**Initiative ID:** NMB-002
**Initiative type:** Conversational Bot
**Business unit:** Human Resources
**Executive sponsor:** Meera Iyer (CHRO Office)
**Delivery lead:** Tomasz Nowak (HR Technology Lead)
**Go-live:** February 2026

## 1. Purpose and Business Case
Answer routine HR policy, leave and payroll questions through chat so that HR advisers can focus on complex cases.

## 2. Scope
All employees in India and the UK. Grievance handling and case management are excluded.

## 3. Target Users and Adoption Approach
Employees self-serve through the intranet and Teams. Usage is naturally seasonal and peaks around payroll cycles and the annual leave window.

## 4. Benefits Measurement Approach
Benefit is deflected tickets multiplied by average cost per ticket, validated by HR Operations. Reported monthly in USD.

## 5. Risks and Dependencies
Policy documents going out of date; sensitive queries that must be escalated to a human; employee privacy expectations.

## 6. Governance and Next Steps
The initiative reports to the AI Portfolio Board each month. Add benefits-enrolment journeys and a handover-to-adviser flow.
