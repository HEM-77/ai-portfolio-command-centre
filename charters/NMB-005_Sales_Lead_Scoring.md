# Project Charter: Sales Lead Scoring (NMB-005)

**Initiative ID:** NMB-005
**Initiative type:** Predictive Model
**Business unit:** Sales
**Executive sponsor:** Rohan Kapoor (Chief Revenue Officer)
**Delivery lead:** Sofia Alvarez (Sales Analytics Lead)
**Go-live:** December 2025

## 1. Purpose and Business Case
Prioritise inbound leads using a predicted conversion likelihood so that sales representatives focus on the most promising opportunities.

## 2. Scope
Inside-sales teams in three regions, with scores shown inside the CRM.

## 3. Target Users and Adoption Approach
Representatives see the score in the CRM. Adoption is measured by representatives who open the score panel each week.

## 4. Benefits Measurement Approach
Benefit is incremental conversion margin attributed through an A/B holdout. Finance reports it as a cumulative running total to date, in USD, rather than as monthly figures.

## 5. Risks and Dependencies
Attribution disputes between sales and marketing; bias in historical training data; representatives ignoring scores.

## 6. Governance and Next Steps
The initiative reports to the AI Portfolio Board each month. Refresh the model with Q4 data and continue the holdout group.
