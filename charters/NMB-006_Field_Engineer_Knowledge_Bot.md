# Project Charter: Field Engineer Knowledge Bot (NMB-006)

**Initiative ID:** NMB-006
**Initiative type:** Conversational Bot
**Business unit:** Operations
**Executive sponsor:** Ingrid Larsen (COO Office)
**Delivery lead:** Vikram Rao (Field Service Digital Lead)
**Go-live:** May 2026

## 1. Purpose and Business Case
Give field engineers instant answers from manuals and repair histories on a mobile device, reducing repeat visits.

## 2. Scope
Field service engineers across six regions using the mobile app.

## 3. Target Users and Adoption Approach
Adoption barriers recorded in the charter include patchy mobile connectivity at customer sites, the lack of an offline mode, and a habit of phoning senior colleagues instead.

## 4. Benefits Measurement Approach
Benefit is avoided repeat visits and reduced travel. Reported monthly in USD.

## 5. Risks and Dependencies
Connectivity gaps; a backlog of manuals still to be digitised; low trust in answers without source references.

## 6. Governance and Next Steps
The initiative reports to the AI Portfolio Board each month. Design an offline mode and set up a champion network in each region.
