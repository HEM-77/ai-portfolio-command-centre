# Project Charter: Demand Forecasting Model (NMB-003)

**Initiative ID:** NMB-003
**Initiative type:** Predictive Model
**Business unit:** Supply Chain
**Executive sponsor:** Arjun Mehta (Head of Supply Chain Planning)
**Delivery lead:** Lena Fischer (Data Science Lead)
**Go-live:** August 2025

## 1. Purpose and Business Case
Improve forecast accuracy for key product families to reduce stock-outs and excess inventory.

## 2. Scope
Regional demand planners running weekly forecast cycles. New-product forecasting is excluded.

## 3. Target Users and Adoption Approach
A small expert group of planners reviews and overrides model forecasts; licences are issued per planner.

## 4. Benefits Measurement Approach
Benefit combines inventory carrying-cost reduction and avoided stock-out revenue loss, validated by Finance. Because inventory reviews run quarterly, benefit is reported quarterly in USD, with quarters counted from the go-live month. Cost is reported monthly.

## 5. Risks and Dependencies
Quality of the promotions calendar; model drift as demand patterns shift; inconsistent planner overrides.

## 6. Governance and Next Steps
The initiative reports to the AI Portfolio Board each month. Move to a monthly retraining cadence. Data sources are the ERP sales history, the promotions calendar and external weather feeds.
