# Project Charter: Predictive Maintenance Model (NMB-009)

**Initiative ID:** NMB-009
**Initiative type:** Predictive Model
**Business unit:** Manufacturing
**Executive sponsor:** Hiroshi Tanaka (VP Manufacturing)
**Delivery lead:** Nadia Haddad (Industrial AI Lead)
**Go-live:** October 2025

## 1. Purpose and Business Case
Predict equipment failures on production lines to cut unplanned downtime and spare-parts waste.

## 2. Scope
Five plants with sensor-equipped lines. Offline legacy lines are excluded.

## 3. Target Users and Adoption Approach
Maintenance engineers and planners use dashboards and alerts during daily shift handovers.

## 4. Benefits Measurement Approach
Benefit is avoided downtime hours plus spare-parts savings. Plant Finance reports in USD thousands, monthly.

## 5. Risks and Dependencies
Sensor coverage gaps; false alarms eroding trust; integration with the maintenance management system.

## 6. Governance and Next Steps
The initiative reports to the AI Portfolio Board each month. Extend to three additional plants once alert precision is reviewed.
