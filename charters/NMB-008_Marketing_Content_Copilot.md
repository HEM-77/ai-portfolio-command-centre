# Project Charter: Marketing Content Copilot (NMB-008)

**Initiative ID:** NMB-008
**Initiative type:** Copilot
**Business unit:** Marketing
**Executive sponsor:** Camille Dubois (CMO Europe)
**Delivery lead:** Jonas Becker (Marketing Technology Lead)
**Go-live:** March 2026

## 1. Purpose and Business Case
Speed up drafting and localisation of campaign copy for European markets.

## 2. Scope
Marketing teams and agency partners in Europe. Paid-media bidding is excluded.

## 3. Target Users and Adoption Approach
Marketers have embraced the tool enthusiastically and usage is broad across teams.

## 4. Benefits Measurement Approach
Benefit is agency spend avoided plus cycle-time reduction. European Finance reports figures in EUR; group reporting converts at a fixed planning rate.

## 5. Risks and Dependencies
Per-seat licence cost is high relative to the benefit delivered; the brand-compliance review step is a bottleneck; copyright status of generated content.

## 6. Governance and Next Steps
The initiative reports to the AI Portfolio Board each month. Renegotiate licensing towards tiered seats and automate the brand-compliance check.
