# Project Charter: Nimbus Service Copilot (NMB-001)

**Initiative ID:** NMB-001
**Initiative type:** Copilot
**Business unit:** Customer Service
**Executive sponsor:** Priya Raman (VP Customer Service)
**Delivery lead:** Daniel Okafor (Programme Lead)
**Go-live:** November 2025

## 1. Purpose and Business Case
Reduce average handling time and raise first-contact resolution by giving customer service agents an AI copilot that drafts replies and surfaces knowledge articles inside the ticketing tool.

## 2. Scope
Tier-1 and Tier-2 agents in the EMEA and North America contact centres, covering email and chat. Voice is out of scope for this phase.

## 3. Target Users and Adoption Approach
Agents are onboarded in cohorts of 200 with floor-walker support. The charter sets a target of 70% weekly adoption by month nine.

## 4. Benefits Measurement Approach
Benefit is agent hours saved multiplied by loaded hourly cost, validated monthly by Finance. Figures are reported in USD, monthly.

## 5. Risks and Dependencies
Answer quality on rare product lines; dependency on knowledge-base freshness; agent trust in suggested replies.

## 6. Governance and Next Steps
The initiative reports to the AI Portfolio Board each month. Pilot voice-call transcripts and add multilingual drafting in the next planning cycle.
