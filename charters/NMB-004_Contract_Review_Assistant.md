# Project Charter: Contract Review Assistant (NMB-004)

**Initiative ID:** NMB-004
**Initiative type:** Copilot
**Business unit:** Legal
**Executive sponsor:** Helena Brandt (General Counsel)
**Delivery lead:** Kwame Mensah (Legal Operations Manager)
**Go-live:** April 2026

## 1. Purpose and Business Case
Accelerate first-pass review of NDAs and supplier contracts by highlighting non-standard clauses and suggesting fallback wording.

## 2. Scope
Legal team and procurement counsel. Litigation documents are out of scope.

## 3. Target Users and Adoption Approach
Lawyers are adopting cautiously. The charter sets a target of 50% weekly adoption, supported by monthly training clinics.

## 4. Benefits Measurement Approach
Benefit is lawyer hours saved on first-pass review plus external counsel spend avoided. Reported monthly in USD.

## 5. Risks and Dependencies
Lawyers distrust unverified clause suggestions; every output needs human review; confidentiality of contract text must be preserved.
